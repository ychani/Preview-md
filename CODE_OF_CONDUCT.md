# Code of Conduct

PreviewMD follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good faith, accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub — reach out to [@ychani](https://github.com/ychani) — rather than in a public thread. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or block participants who violate this code, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
